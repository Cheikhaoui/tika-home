# Arrival and departure

## Check-in and check-out times
Check-in is from 15:00 and check-out is until 11:00. Early check-in or late check-out can sometimes be arranged when the house is free the night before or after: ask the owner. Luggage can usually be left at the house before check-in or after check-out.

## Finding the house
Cars cannot enter the narrow lanes of the medina. Guests are met at the nearest drop-off point, about 3 minutes' walk from the house, and accompanied to the door. Precise directions and a meeting point are sent before arrival. A porter with a cart can carry heavy luggage for a small tip.

## Airport transfer
A private transfer from Marrakech Menara airport can be booked on request. The drive takes about 20 minutes. The driver waits at arrivals with a name sign. Mention your flight number in your booking message or on WhatsApp.

## Keys and arrival contact
The owner or a member of the team welcomes guests in person, shows the house and gives the keys. For late arrivals after 22:00, tell us in advance so we can organise it.
