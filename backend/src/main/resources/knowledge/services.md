# Services

## Breakfast
A traditional Moroccan breakfast can be served on the roof terrace on request: msemen pancakes, bread, honey, jam, eggs, fruit, juice, mint tea and coffee. It is an extra charge per person per day; ask the owner for the current price.

## Cleaning during the stay
The house is cleaned before arrival. For stays of a week or more, a mid-stay cleaning with fresh linen and towels is included. Extra cleaning can be arranged on request.

## Activities and excursions
The owner can help organise a hammam, a cooking class, a day trip to the Atlas Mountains or the Agafay desert, and official city guides. Ask in advance during busy seasons.
