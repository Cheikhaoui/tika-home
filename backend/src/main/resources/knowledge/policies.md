# Booking, payment and house rules

## How booking works
Guests send a booking request on the website, with no payment at that point. The owner confirms or declines within 24 hours by email. After confirmation, the owner explains how to pay. Availability and prices on the website are always up to date.

## Payment
After the owner confirms, payment details are sent by email. Payment options are agreed with the owner directly. Online card payment on the website is not available yet.

## Cancellation policy
Cancellation is free up to 14 days before arrival. This policy is a placeholder and must be confirmed with the owner for each booking.

## Pets
Pets are not allowed in the house, including dogs and cats. Assistance dogs are welcome: tell the owner in advance.

## Smoking
Smoking is not allowed inside the house. Guests may smoke on the roof terrace.

## Parties, noise and quiet hours
Parties and events are not allowed. Quiet hours are from 22:00 to 08:00 out of respect for the neighbours in this residential lane.

## Children
Children and babies are welcome. A baby cot is available on request. The plunge pool is not fenced, so children must be supervised at all times.

## Prices and minimum stay
The price depends on the dates: there are seasonal prices, for example around Christmas and New Year, and a minimum number of nights that can be longer in high season. A cleaning fee is added once per stay. The exact total is shown on the website when dates are selected.
