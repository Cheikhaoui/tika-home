# Neighbourhood and Marrakech

## Distances
Jemaa el-Fna square is a 10 minute walk. The souks are 5 minutes away. The Koutoubia Mosque is a 12 minute walk. Jardin Majorelle is about 15 minutes by taxi. Marrakech Menara airport is about 20 minutes by car.

## Safety in the medina
The neighbourhood is residential and quiet. The main lanes to Jemaa el-Fna are lively and generally safe in the evening, as is the square itself, which is busiest at night. As in any busy place, keep an eye on bags and phones. Ignore people offering to guide you if you did not ask; the owner can recommend official guides.

## Parking
There is no parking at the house because cars cannot enter the medina. Guarded public car parks are a few minutes' walk away, around 30 to 50 dirhams per day. Taxis are the easiest way to get around outside the medina.

## Shops and food nearby
A small grocery shop, a bakery and a pharmacy are within 3 minutes' walk. Many restaurants and cafes are near Jemaa el-Fna. The owner shares a list of favourite restaurants on arrival.

## Weather
Marrakech is hot and dry from June to September, often above 35 degrees. Spring and autumn are mild and the most pleasant seasons. Winter days are sunny and mild but nights can be cold, around 5 degrees.
