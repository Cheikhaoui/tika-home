## Overview
Tika Home is a traditional riad in the Marrakech medina, rented in its entirety: guests have the whole house to themselves. It has 3 bedrooms, 4 beds and 2 bathrooms, and sleeps up to 6 guests. The rooms open onto a bright central courtyard with a plunge pool, orange trees and zellige tiles. There is a roof terrace with sun loungers and a dining area.

## Bedrooms and beds
There are 3 bedrooms: two with a double bed and one with two single beds that can be joined. A baby cot is available on request at no cost. Beds are made on arrival with fresh linen. There are 2 bathrooms with walk-in showers, shared between the bedrooms.

## Pool
The plunge pool is in the courtyard. It is about 4 by 2 metres and 1.4 metres deep. It is not heated, so it is most pleasant from May to October. The pool is not fenced: children must be supervised at all times. Pool towels are provided.

## Kitchen
The kitchen is fully equipped: oven, hob, fridge with freezer, microwave, coffee machine, kettle, toaster, dishes and cooking utensils. Basic supplies are provided: salt, pepper, oil, tea, coffee and sugar.

## Comfort and connectivity
Fast fibre Wi-Fi works in the whole house, including the roof terrace. Every bedroom and the living room have air conditioning with heating, useful in winter nights which can be cold in Marrakech. There is a washing machine, an iron and a drying rack. The house does not have a dishwasher or a television.
